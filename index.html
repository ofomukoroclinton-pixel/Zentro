Help & Safety Centre

Practical guidance for using Zentro responsibly. Never share your password or verification codes with another member.

🛡️ Protect your account

Use a unique password, keep your email account secure, and sign out on devices you do not control. Zentro staff will never need your password.

🚩 Report and block

If a member harasses you, impersonates someone, sends suspicious links, or makes you uncomfortable, use the available Report and Block actions. Do not send money to people you have only met online.

🔒 Protect your privacy

Share only information you are comfortable making visible. Do not post your home address, private documents, passwords, or precise live location.

✅ Understand badges

A badge should only appear when Zentro has authorised that account. A Founder & CEO label identifies the founder account; ordinary member verification requires an actual review. Email confirmation alone does not prove a person's identity.

🤝 Community standards

Be respectful, do not impersonate others, do not spam or threaten people, and do not post content that violates others' privacy. Report concerns instead of escalating conflict.

♿ Accessibility

Zentro respects your device's reduced-motion preference and provides visible keyboard focus indicators. If an important action is hard to use, report the issue so it can be improved.

Need more help?

Use the in-app Report option for safety concerns. Official platform notices appear in Announcements. Support contact details should be added here once Zentro has a dedicated, monitored support address.

This in-app guide is practical safety guidance, not a substitute for Zentro's final legal terms or privacy policy. Those documents should be reviewed and published before broad public launch.
